# Self-hosting

The backend is open source at [github.com/cheddatech/CheddaBoards](https://github.com/cheddatech/CheddaBoards). Self-hosting means deploying your own canister on the Internet Computer, building your own HTTP proxy in front of it, and setting the verifier principal.
